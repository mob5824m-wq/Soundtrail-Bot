                  Tickets

                  The panel offers Technical issue, Account help and General question. Each member can have one open ticket at a time. Opening one creates a private channel for them and the staff role. Members can request urgent review; staff can claim, assign or change priority with /manage ticket commands. Use /manage ticket report for volume and first-response times. Closing saves a transcript below, then deletes the channel. Set the Discord channel category under Settings, and an idle-close limit there if you want quiet tickets closed for you.

                        Role menus

                        A role menu is one message with a dropdown. Members pick the roles they want from it. Roles they leave unticked are taken away, so a menu can also remove a role.

                            Settings

                            Retention removes local transcript copies and download links after the chosen period. Files already sent to a Discord staff-log channel are not deleted automatically.

                            Support hours can cross midnight. Leave both times blank for always-on support. Outside hours, new and reopened tickets include this message.

                            Quiet hours hold scheduled and repeating posts until the window ends. Use 24-hour times in the server's timezone. Leave both empty to turn them off.

                            Staff role: once set, only its holders (and administrators) can use staff tools. Leave it empty to fall back to Manage Server.
